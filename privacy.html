Privacy · CodePrice

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This notice explains how CodePrice handles information when you use the CodePrice Chrome extension or visit this website. CodePrice is operated from the United Kingdom. For privacy enquiries, use the contact email below.

In short: CodePrice scans a product page only after you open the extension. The scan runs locally in your browser; page information is not sent to CodePrice or an analytics or advertising service.

Information the extension accesses

When you open the CodePrice popup, it automatically reads page text and relevant product price information in the active tab to identify a product price, currency, advertised promotion, and relevant offer terms. You can choose “Check this page” to scan again. It does not scan tabs in the background or access your browsing history.

How information is used

The page information is used to calculate and display the estimate you requested. The scan is processed on your device. CodePrice does not send page text, prices, coupon codes, or browsing activity to the publisher, a CodePrice server, analytics provider, advertising service, or other third party. The scan result is displayed in the popup and is not retained after the popup closes.

Website visits

This informational website is intended to operate without analytics, advertising trackers, or non-essential cookies. Your browser and website host may process routine technical data needed to deliver and secure the page, under their own terms. No CodePrice analytics or advertising scripts are included in this site.

Permissions

  • activeTab: lets CodePrice read the page you chose to scan after you open the popup.
  • scripting: runs the local price scan in that active tab.

Sharing and Chrome Web Store Limited Use

CodePrice does not sell, share, or disclose scanned page information to the publisher or other parties. These details are used only for the disclosed price-estimation feature. CodePrice does not use this information for advertising, creditworthiness, lending, or sale to data brokers. CodePrice’s use of webpage content and related information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Lawful basis and international use

Where data-protection law applies to personal data processed by CodePrice, the extension’s scan is user-initiated and processed locally to provide the requested feature. CodePrice does not receive or retain the scanned page information. The extension is intended for users internationally; local privacy rights may vary by location.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, data portability, or object to processing. Because CodePrice does not receive or retain scanned page information, the publisher may not hold a copy to retrieve. UK users can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office; people in the EEA can contact their local data-protection authority.
