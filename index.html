Know the price
before the basket.

Ever wanted to know what an item might cost after a discount without doing the maths in your head—or waiting until it’s in your basket to find out? With CodePrice, you can see an estimate while you shop. Here’s how it works.

01 / FIND

Open a product page

Choose a product page in Chrome and open CodePrice when you want to check it.

02 / ESTIMATE

See the price after the code

When it can identify the product price and offer, CodePrice shows an estimate and saving.

03 / CHECK

Confirm with the retailer

Retailer rules decide the final amount. Check eligibility, delivery, tax and exclusions before paying.

Real-world caveats.

Promotions don’t work the same way on every item. Some shops apply discounts only in the basket or exclude selected products. CodePrice shows an estimate when it can read the relevant details; the retailer’s checkout is the final word.
Quick answers

A little more detail.

Does CodePrice work on every shop?

Retailer pages vary. CodePrice is designed to scan the active product page, but it may not recognise every layout, dynamic price or offer format.

Will it know if a code definitely works?

No. It estimates from price and promotion information visible on the page. Only the retailer can confirm eligibility and the final checkout total.

What information does CodePrice use?

When opened, it reads product price and promotion information from the selected active tab and processes the scan locally. See the Privacy Policy for details.
